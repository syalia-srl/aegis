- **A workflow run now explains itself above its steps.** An expanded card in
  the transcript, the viewer or a docked pane carries a short recap of what
  finished, what is running, what remains and what the run waits on, written by
  the agent `recap:` names. It refreshes only while a card intersects a visible
  tab; folding a long map, a clock tick or a second tab on the same state costs
  nothing, and reopening shows the saved text while one catch-up call runs.
