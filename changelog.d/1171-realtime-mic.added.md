- **Speak to a session in realtime.** Hold the mic and slide to Realtime to send blocks of transcribed speech while you talk. The status bar keeps the source session and Stop mic visible as you move around. If speech recognition fails, Retry speech recovers the retained audio. Refused sends retain selectable text and can be retried. Tap the mic for an editable draft, or slide to Send audio for a recording.
