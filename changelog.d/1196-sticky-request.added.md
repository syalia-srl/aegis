- **The latest request stays over the transcript while you read the reply.**
  Once your last message scrolls out above the transcript, its text returns at
  the top of the pane, two lines at a time and expandable in place, and leaves
  when the original row comes back into view. Reading a long reply no longer
  costs your place to check what you asked, and a reopened long transcript pins
  the request even before its row is mounted.
