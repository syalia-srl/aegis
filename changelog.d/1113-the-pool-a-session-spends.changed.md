- **A session is measured, guarded, cooled and billed on the pool it spends.**
  A Claude Code session whose process talked to OpenCode Go was counted against
  Claude: Claude's gauge, Claude's quota notices, cooling aimed at Claude's
  models, and a bill at Opus prices. aegis now resolves a session's pool — the
  one its record names, else what its harness and model imply — and the card
  carries it, so the sidebar draws the account's own gauge. A pool fall cools
  that account alone, and the Claude-only paths (its rate-limit reading, its
  turn-end refresh and its sign-in card) act only for a session whose pool is
  `claude`.
